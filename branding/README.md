# StagePilot — icona originale

Simbolo: una S azzurra con un triangolo Play nello spazio negativo centrale, su grafite. Sostituisce le cinque barre arancioni ereditate da Backline e il simbolo radio usato nello studio web.

Creato con lo strumento imagegen integrato; esportazioni con solo ridimensionamento per i diversi dispositivi. Il master originale è `stagepilot-icon-master.png`.

- Android: `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/stagepilot_launcher.png`, adattiva con margine di sicurezza, usata anche nella testata interna.
- Web: `web/public/stagepilot-icon.png`, versione 512, favicon PNG/ICO e icona touch. Asset locali, disponibili senza rete.

## Prompt finale

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production Android launcher icon and matching web favicon for StagePilot, a simple professional concert setlist and rehearsal app for musicians. Create ONE original square 1024x1024 app icon, front-on, perfectly crisp, premium modern flat graphic, not a mockup. Full bleed solid very dark graphite #101114 background, no external border, no rounded square tile, no framing or perspective. Center a bold memorable ice-blue #7fb7ff emblem that fuses a stylized capital S with a forward play triangle cut out in negative space: two broad flowing ribbon strokes make a strong musical S silhouette, the central right-facing triangular opening suggests starting the performance. The shape should feel energetic, balanced, elegant and instantly legible at 32 pixels. Limit to large simple geometric forms, smooth precise curves, flat blue with only an extremely subtle lighter upper face if useful; no glow, tiny detail, shadows, gradients, lettering, wordmarks, microphone, equalizer bars or radio-tower symbol. This must be clearly different from an orange equalizer icon. Keep all blue artwork inside the central 60% of the canvas with generous identical graphite padding, for Android adaptive icon safe area. Single icon only, no alternatives, no text, no watermark.
